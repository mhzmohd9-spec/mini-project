Gesture Reference Booklet

1. Hello / Greeting

Extend open hand with palm facing forward and move side-to-side gently.

2. Thank You

Touch fingers to chin and move hand forward towards person.

3. Yes / Affirmative

Form a fist and nod it up and down slowly at wrist.

4. Please

Place open hand over chest and move in circular motion.
